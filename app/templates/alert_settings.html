🔊 Test the spoken alert

Phones and computers block sound until you tap the page once. Press this to switch it on and hear exactly what an announcement sounds like. Turn your volume up first.

You should hear a chime, then: "{{ current_user.name }}, this is a test announcement. 3 patients are waiting for your attention."

Hear nothing? Check the phone is not on silent, that your browser is allowed to play sound for this site, and that "Speak alerts aloud" above is ticked.
