Flow — who does what (clean review):
1. Reception — takes details, records insurance/policy number, special needs, next of kin. Detects priority lane (elderly 60+, pregnant, child <5, wheelchair). Sends to Billing.
2. Billing — raises bill for folder + blood sugar test. Shows patient + policy number. Sends to Pay Point.
3. Megalex / Pay Point — collects payment, records receipt. Sends to HIMS.
4. HIMS — most appropriate to push to HIMS is Pay Point after payment. HIMS sees PAID list and opens folder (creates hospital number, visit, priority). Sends to Triage.
5. Triage — blood sugar test, places patient to clinic according to available doctors, category, priority lane first.
6. Consulting Room — doctor clicks "Ready to consult", sees Call Room Queue with priority first, journey time estimate.
7. LAHSMA — simple clearance desk (just tracking, like Billing). Shows patient + policy number.
8. Onward board — shows all pending post-consult destinations with priority first.
9. TV — shows live queue, priority lane, estimated wait and total journey time, Nigeria voices 2M2F recycled daily EN+YO.
