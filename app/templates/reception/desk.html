Flow — who does what (clean review):
1. Reception — takes details, records insurance/policy number, special needs, next of kin. Sends to Billing.
2. Billing — raises bill for folder + blood sugar test. Shows patient + policy number. Sends to Pay Point.
3. Megalex / Pay Point — collects payment, records receipt. Sends to HIMS.
4. HIMS — most appropriate to push to HIMS is Pay Point after payment. HIMS sees PAID list and opens folder (creates hospital number, visit). Sends to Triage. Also handles returning patients by searching folder.
5. Triage — blood sugar test, places patient to OPD/SOPD/MOPD/EMERGENCY according to available doctors, category, day.
6. Consulting Room — doctor clicks \"Ready to consult\", sees Call Room Queue, calls in, finishes and pushes to 1-3 destinations: LAHSMA / Billing / Megalex / Laboratory / Pharmacy / Emergency.
7. LAHSMA — simple clearance desk (just tracking, like Billing). Shows patient + policy number, issues clearance. LAHSMA has their own web app for real policy management.
8. Onward board — shows all pending post-consult destinations in one view.
